SELECT region, SUM(revenue) AS total FROM orders WHERE order_date >= '2026-07-01' GROUP BY region ORDER BY total ASC;

↓ 12%
REGION WEST
↓ 24%
↓
INSIGHT
Insight: Penurunan terutama berasal dari transaksi region West, khususnya kategori elektronik yang turun tajam sejak awal Juli.
